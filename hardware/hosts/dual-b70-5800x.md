## Notes

- Peer-to-peer works on this box despite community defaults of `CCL_TOPO_P2P_ACCESS=0`. See [findings/bmg-pcie-p2p-works.md](../../findings/bmg-pcie-p2p-works.md).
- AMD desktop platforms have reports of ASPM-related link issues with Arc cards; check ASPM state before chasing driver bugs.
